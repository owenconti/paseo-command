One fleet, two sound palettes

Original draft cues for Galaxy and Lake Country, draft 2. Each event has its own shape (rhythm, contour, length, register and timbre); each world plays that shape on its own instruments. Playback starts only when you press a player control. Each player begins at 35% volume; use a comfortable system level. Starting a cue pauses the others.

These assets passed file-level and measured-distinctness checks but have not been auditioned. Listen down each column to judge whether every event is recognisable, and across each row to judge whether the two worlds still mean the same thing.
